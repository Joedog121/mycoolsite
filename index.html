Common Questions

FAQ

Do you handle electrical panel upgrades in Seattle?

Yes, we upgrade electrical panels throughout Seattle and the surrounding areas. Contact us to schedule an inspection and quote.

How quickly can you install an EV charging station?

Timelines depend on the location, panel capacity, and charger type. Reach out and we’ll provide availability and a project estimate.

What's included in a home electrical safety inspection?

We inspect key components such as wiring, outlets, panels, grounding, and visible safety concerns to help identify issues before they become bigger problems.

Can you wire homes for remodels or new construction?

Yes. We provide electrical work for remodels, upgrades, and new construction projects, and can coordinate with builders or contractors as needed.

Do I need a panel upgrade before installing an EV charger?

Not always. We can evaluate your current electrical system and determine whether your panel has enough available capacity for the charger you want.

How do I know if my home may need rewiring?

Frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, outdated wiring, warm outlets, or inconsistent power can all be signs that your home may need electrical updates.
