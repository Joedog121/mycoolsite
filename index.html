Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve Seattle and surrounding areas?

Do you provide panel upgrades?

Can you install a home EV charger?

Yes, we install EV charging stations and can check whether your current electrical system is ready for one.

Do you handle remodel wiring?

Yes, we provide wiring and electrical work for remodels, home updates, and additions.

How do I know if I need rewiring?

Common signs include flickering lights, breaker trips, warm outlets, old wiring, or unreliable power.
