Common Questions

FAQ

Do you handle electrical panel upgrades in Seattle?

Yes, we upgrade electrical panels throughout Seattle and the surrounding areas. Call us today to schedule your panel inspection.

How quickly can you install an EV charging station?

Installation timelines vary by location and setup complexity. We serve Bellevue, Redmond, and King County — contact us for a specific quote and availability.

What's involved in a home electrical safety inspection?

We inspect wiring, outlets, panels, and grounding to identify hazards. We're available for inspections in Tacoma, Seattle, and Pierce County.

Can you wire a home during new construction?

We specialize in new construction wiring across King County, Snohomish County, and Skagit County. Reach out early to coordinate with your builder.

What should I do before scheduling a generator install?

Note your home's power needs and fuel preference. We install generators in Everett, Renton, and surrounding areas — contact us to discuss options.

How long does a full home rewire take?

Rewire duration depends on home size and scope. We serve Ballard, Capitol Hill, and Queen Anne — we'll provide a timeline after your consultation.
