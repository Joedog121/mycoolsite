Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle electrical panel upgrades in Seattle?

Yes, we provide panel upgrades throughout Seattle and nearby areas. We’re happy to take a look and provide a quote.

How quickly can you install an EV charger?

Timing depends on your panel capacity, charger type, and installation location. Contact us and we’ll let you know current availability.

What’s included in a home electrical safety inspection?

We check important parts of your home’s electrical system, including wiring, outlets, panel condition, grounding, and visible safety concerns.

Can you help with remodels and rewiring?

Yes. We provide electrical work for remodels, upgrades, rewiring, and new residential projects.

Do I need a panel upgrade before getting an EV charger?

Not always. We can inspect your current electrical system and let you know whether your home has the available capacity.

How do I know if my home may need rewiring?

Warning signs can include flickering lights, breaker trips, outdated wiring, warm outlets, or inconsistent power in parts of the home.
