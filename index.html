FAQ

Questions We Hear Often

Do you work on homes in Seattle and nearby areas?

Yes. We serve homeowners in Seattle and many nearby communities. If you're not sure whether you're in our service area, just give us a call.

Can you help if my breakers keep tripping or lights are flickering?

Yes. Those can be signs of electrical issues that should be checked out. We can inspect the problem and recommend the safest next step.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. We can look at your current panel and let you know whether it has enough capacity for the charger you want.

Do you do wiring for remodels?

Yes. We handle electrical work for remodels, upgrades, and other home improvement projects.

What’s included in a home electrical safety inspection?

We inspect important parts of your system, such as the panel, wiring, outlets, grounding, and visible safety concerns.

How do I know if my home may need rewiring?

Frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, old wiring, warm outlets, or unreliable power can all be signs that your home may need electrical updates.
