Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Erickson Electric LLC respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us through our online quote form, by phone, or by email.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if provided
  • Details about your electrical project or service request
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message

We may also receive limited technical information automatically when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general usage data. This information may be processed by our website hosting provider or third-party services used on the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to quote requests, service inquiries, and messages
  • Call, email, or otherwise communicate with you about your project
  • Schedule service appointments
  • Provide residential electrical services
  • Improve our website, customer service, and business operations
  • Maintain records related to inquiries, estimates, and completed work
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, or business obligations

3. Contact Form and Third-Party Processing

Our website uses FormSubmit to process contact form submissions. When you submit the form, the information you enter may be transmitted through FormSubmit before being delivered to Erickson Electric LLC by email.

By submitting the contact form, you understand that your information may be processed by FormSubmit according to its own policies and practices.

4. Phone, Email, and Service Communications

If you contact us or request a quote, we may use your phone number or email address to respond to your inquiry, provide estimates, schedule service, ask follow-up questions, or communicate about your project.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your contact information for unrelated third-party marketing.

5. Google Fonts and Google Maps

Our website uses Google Fonts to display website typography and Google Maps to show our general service location. When these services load, Google may collect information such as your IP address, browser details, device information, and interaction data.

Your use of Google services is subject to Google’s own privacy practices. You can review Google’s Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

6. Website Hosting

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and server logs, as part of providing website hosting and security.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Erickson Electric LLC does not currently use its own advertising cookies, analytics pixels, or behavioral tracking tools on this website. However, third-party services embedded or loaded on the website, such as Google Maps, Google Fonts, FormSubmit, or GitHub Pages, may use cookies, logs, or similar technologies according to their own policies.

8. How We Share Information

We may share information only as reasonably necessary to operate our business and provide services, including with:

  • Service providers that help operate our website or process form submissions
  • Email, phone, scheduling, or business record systems we use
  • Contractors, inspectors, permitting authorities, or suppliers when needed for your project
  • Legal, regulatory, or governmental authorities if required by law

We do not sell your personal information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted to us. However, no website, email system, or online transmission method is completely secure. Please avoid submitting highly sensitive information through the website contact form.

10. Data Retention

We may keep inquiry, estimate, communication, and service-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal or tax obligations.

11. Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update, correct, or delete personal information you have provided to us, subject to any legal, accounting, safety, warranty, or legitimate business recordkeeping obligations.

12. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for homeowners and adults seeking residential electrical services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years old.

13. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
